    Quick reminders

    • Face to face. Hold the screen beside your face so she can watch your mouth and the picture together.
    • Say it with the app. Say each word yourself, slowly, right along with the voice.
    • Wait. The dots show her turn. Look at her and smile while you wait.
    • Every attempt counts. “Ba” for “ball” is a win. Say the word back the right way (“Yes, ball!”) without asking her to repeat it.
    • Short and often. 3–5 minutes at a time, a few times a day. Stop while it’s still fun.
    • Add it to the home screen. In Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen.” On Android Chrome, open the ⋮ menu and choose “Add to Home screen.” It will open full screen like an app.
